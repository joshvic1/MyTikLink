# MyTikLink V2.1 — Clear Business Workspace

## Relationship to V2

V2.1 preserves V2's labelled sidebar, predictable page hierarchy, dedicated Links, Pages, Store, Orders, Leads, Analytics and Tracking destinations, consolidated Settings, global Create action, responsive shell, semantic status system, and plain-English interface. It evolves these foundations through adaptive dashboard states, selective surfaces, operational split views, contextual drawers, stronger mobile actions, richer feedback, and a more recognizable dark workspace anchor.

## Philosophy

V2.1 is designed for the business owner who wants to complete a task, not learn software terminology. Every screen establishes location, purpose, primary action, and recommended next step before presenting secondary information.

The visual system combines a dark, compact business navigation with a calm neutral workspace. Violet identifies action and selection, green communicates working/live, amber communicates attention, and red is reserved for destructive or failed states. Personality comes from consistent hierarchy, compact geometry, asymmetric action panels, direct language, and responsive operational workflows—not gradients or decoration.

## Core UX rules

1. The page title states where the user is.
2. Supporting copy explains the page in one sentence.
3. One descriptive primary action is visible near the title.
4. The next recommended action is visually prioritized.
5. Technical language is translated into business outcomes.
6. Advanced controls remain available behind explicit disclosure.
7. Empty states explain value and provide an immediate action.
8. Feedback identifies what happened and whether work was saved.
9. Mobile layouts prioritize orders, creation, pages, and navigation within thumb reach.
10. Cards are reserved for focused tasks, summaries, and temporary context.

## Recognizable design details

- A dark business rail gives the workspace a stable visual anchor.
- Recommended-next-step panels use a narrow violet edge and concise action copy.
- Status combines a coloured dot with explicit language.
- Operational rows carry their primary action without requiring a detail screen.
- Mobile uses a raised central Create action and full-height task sheets.

## Safety

V2.1 uses local mock data and does not connect to production APIs, authentication, payments, databases, or environment configuration.
