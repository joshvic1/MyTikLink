# V2.1 — Business command centre

V2.1 keeps the same plain-language UX foundation but presents it as a premium operating environment. Its hierarchy is editorial and operational: the dashboard opens with a Priority Brief, business metrics are compact, and action queues remain close to the work they affect.

Navigation is grouped as Command, Create, Operate, and Intelligence. The dark anchor rail, warm workspace, acid-lime signal colour, square geometry, serif display typography, and offset action shadows create a memorable identity without weakening comprehension.

V2.1 is designed for returning users managing several workflows. It is denser than V2, but progressive disclosure, explicit statuses, descriptive buttons, and mobile cards keep it approachable.
