# MyTikLink V2.3 — Familiar SaaS, MyTikLink identity

V2.3 uses the conventions people expect from a polished technology product: a light labelled sidebar, searchable top bar, consistent cards, management tables, status chips, settings tabs, responsive drawers and predictable mobile navigation.

Its identity comes from restrained violet edge markers, dark high-contrast action panels, subtle striped signal geometry, sharper data surfaces, contextual next steps and plain-English business copy. Familiarity reduces learning effort; selective signatures prevent the interface from feeling like an off-the-shelf template.
