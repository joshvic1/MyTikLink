# V2 — Guided clarity

V2 is the simplest expression of MyTikLink: a calm, task-first workspace for people who do not speak SaaS or advertising jargon. The home screen prioritizes the one action that matters now, followed by a small set of relevant actions, setup progress, performance in plain language, and recent work.

Navigation is grouped by intent: Start, Build, Customers, and Performance. Primary actions use direct verbs and high contrast. Technical controls remain behind advanced disclosure. Lists expose the useful action on the row, while mobile turns operational tables into thumb-friendly cards.

The blue-and-neutral visual system is intentionally restrained. It uses predictable geometry, visible focus, literal labels, moderate density, and one dominant action per region.
