# MyTikLink V2.2 — Command Canvas

V2.2 is a distinct horizontal-workspace concept. It replaces the permanent conventional sidebar with a top command bar and a compact workspace switcher. A warm canvas, editorial numbering, sharp section bands, large operational type and high-contrast cobalt/ink actions create a recognizable identity without gradients or visual effects.

The design remains non-technical: every page states its purpose, its next action, and the business outcome. Important work appears before reporting. Drawers handle short creation tasks, split views handle orders, dedicated canvases handle builders, and mobile uses a central Create action.

Primary actions use high-contrast cobalt or ink. Secondary actions use outlined white controls. Destructive actions remain visually separate. Status always combines text and colour.
